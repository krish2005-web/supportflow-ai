# Payment Issues Support Policy

## Failed Payment but Money Deducted

When a customer reports that a payment failed but money was deducted:

1. Collect the transaction reference number or order ID.
2. Ask for the transaction date and approximate time.
3. Ask for the transaction amount.
4. Identify the payment method (UPI, card, or net banking).
5. Check the transaction status through the payment verification process.

## Payment Status

Possible payment statuses:

- SUCCESS: Payment was successfully completed.
- FAILED: Payment was unsuccessful.
- PENDING: Payment status is not yet confirmed.

## Troubleshooting

- Do not assume that a failed payment has automatically been reversed.
- Do not promise a refund without transaction verification.
- If payment status is unclear, escalate the issue to the payment support team.
- If duplicate deductions are reported, escalate for transaction investigation.

## Customer Communication

Explain that the payment needs to be verified before confirming its outcome.

Never invent refund timelines or guarantee a resolution time.
